In one line

Tideway is Flight Control for a container port, except every path you draw stays on the board.

In 100 words

Tideway is a one-finger harbor game for iPhone. Ships arrive at gates on the edge of the sea; you drag a lane to a berth and they follow it. The lane stays, so lanes branch and merge into a network, and ships route through it by the real rules of the road — the vessel with another on its starboard side gives way. Cranes unload into yards, trucks and trains carry the cargo away, and when one link jams the whole harbor feels it. Three-minute days, one of three upgrades after each, and a new twist every day that cuts across what you already built.
