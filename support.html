Support

How do I draw a lane?

Drag with one finger from a gate pill at the edge of the sea to a berth on the quay. The lane stays on the board and every ship of that colour follows it.

How do I branch or merge?

Drag from any point on a lane you already drew to a second berth, and the ships route through the junction. Drag from another gate onto an existing lane to merge into it.

How do I remove a lane?

Press and hold it for half a second. You get all of its ink back, and anything that branched off it goes too. The starter road is a gift and cannot be removed.

A ship has stopped. Why?

Look at the chip under the score. It names the cargo and the cause: its gate lane is jammed, its berth is busy, its yard is full, or it is giving way at a crossing. Ships never collide; they wait.

What is the pill with an arrow?

A crossing. The arrow points along the lane that has right of way, by the real rule: the ship with the other on its starboard side gives way. Tap the pill to take harbor control and the other lane goes first.

My yard is full and the crane has stopped.

Clear it. Draw another road from the exit, lay rail, or flick a box straight out of the yard with a short swipe toward its exit. Quick flicks in a row chain for a bonus.

What is the Daily Harbor?

One seed for everyone, every day. Your first run counts for the Game Center board; after that you can replay it freely.

I bought Tideway Plus and it is not showing.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not appear, make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that bought it, then write to us.

Accessibility

Every cargo has a shape as well as a colour, so the game is playable in greyscale. VoiceOver reads the gates, berths, yards, exits and crossings, and lanes can be drawn by double-tapping a gate then a berth. There is a night board, a left-handed HUD, Reduce Motion support and a slow mode.

Still stuck?

Write to us and say which iPhone you have, which iOS version, and what you were doing. The email address is on the App Store listing under Developer Website and in the App Store's support link for Tideway.
