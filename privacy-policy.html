Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Tideway collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of ours. Your scores, settings and purchases stay on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics, night board, left-handed HUD, reduce motion, slow mode.
  • Your best score, how many runs you have played, and the day you reached.
  • Whether you own Tideway Plus.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Apple's services

ServiceWhat goes to itWhen
Game CenterYour score on the Daily Harbor and Season boards, and achievement progress.Only if you are signed in to Game Center. The game plays in full without it.
App StoreThe in-app purchase, handled by Apple's StoreKit. We never see a payment detail.Only if you buy Tideway Plus or restore it.

Those are Apple's services and Apple's privacy policy covers them.

Advertising

This version of Tideway contains no advertising SDK and makes no ad request. If advertising is ever added, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated in the same release, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

Children

Tideway is rated 4+. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
